## 6. Hazards observed this session

- **A probe that counts positions cannot predict a peak-selection change.**
  Regrouping and cross-part normalisation produce collateral far from the
  change. Predict direct effects, then attribute every change after the run.
- **"Permissive" is not a safe property under grouping.** Unmasking a peak can
  remove a different boundary (F1c(b): −13 GT).
- **Exact-match and audit both miss unreviewed extras.** Report the extras
  count and generate a review list for any change that adds boundaries.
